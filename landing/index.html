What We Do

Services We Offer

From GST filings to company registration — we handle your compliance so you can focus on growing your business.

🧾
GST Registration & Filing
GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9 filings. New registration and amendments. Monthly, quarterly, or annual — we handle it all.
📄
Income Tax Return (ITR)
ITR filing for individuals, firms, companies. Tax planning, notices, refunds and 26AS reconciliation.
🔍
Statutory & Tax Audit
Statutory audits under Companies Act, tax audits under Section 44AB. Proper documentation and report filing.
🏢
Company & LLP Registration
Private Limited, LLP, OPC, Partnership registration. ROC filings, MCA compliance and annual returns.
💰
TDS Compliance
TDS deduction, challan payment, quarterly returns (26Q, 24Q). Form 16 / 16A generation for your employees and vendors.
📊
Bookkeeping & Accounting
Monthly bookkeeping, P&L, Balance Sheet. Tally, Zoho Books or any software. MIS reports for business decisions.
